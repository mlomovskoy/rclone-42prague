What this is

rclone-42prague is a personal, non-commercial configuration of rclone, an open-source command-line program for managing files on cloud storage. It is used by a single individual to keep a local folder in sync with their own Google Drive account across the computers they work on, including machines at the 42 Prague campus.

How it works

  • The user signs in with their own Google account and grants access.
  • rclone then copies files between that Google Drive account and a folder on the user's computer.
  • Everything runs locally on the user's own machine. There is no server, no hosted backend, and no website that processes files.

Google account access

This app requests a single Google Drive scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This is the most limited Drive scope available. It grants access only to files and folders the app itself creates or that the user explicitly opens with it. The app cannot see or touch the rest of the user's Drive.

Who it is for

This is a private tool built and used by its author. It is not offered as a product or service to other people, and there is nothing to sign up for.
